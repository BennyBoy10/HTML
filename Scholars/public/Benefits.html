Scholarship Benefits

Global Scholars is designed to remove financial barriers that prevent talented Nigerian students from studying abroad. The program covers core academic and living costs, so scholars can focus fully on their education, personal growth, and long-term career goals.

What We Cover

We cover the essential costs of studying abroad, including tuition, accommodation, living support, health coverage, and key academic expenses required throughout the program.

Complete Tuition Coverage

Your program tuition for the entire duration at our partner institutions worldwide is included. This applies to undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs.

  • Full program tuition coverage
  • All academic fees included
  • Duration matched to your program

Accommodation Support

Secure and comfortable housing near your campus, with essential utilities and internet connectivity provided throughout your study period.

  • On-campus or nearby housing
  • Utilities and internet included
  • Safe, student-focused environment

Living Stipend

A monthly allowance for essential expenses including food, transportation, study materials, and personal needs, adjusted to your host country's cost of living.

  • Monthly living allowance
  • Country-specific adjustments
  • Covers essential daily expenses

International Travel

Round-trip economy airfare from Nigeria to your host country, with airport transfers and travel coordination support upon arrival and departure.

  • Round-trip airfare
  • Airport transfer assistance
  • Travel coordination support

Visa & Processing

All visa application costs, embassy fees, and document processing expenses. Our team provides guidance throughout the visa application process.

  • All visa application fees
  • Document processing support
  • Application guidance

Health Insurance

Comprehensive medical coverage throughout your study period, including emergency care, routine medical needs, and mental health support services.

  • Full medical coverage
  • Emergency and routine care
  • Mental health support included
