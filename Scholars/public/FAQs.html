Common Questions

Browse through our most frequently asked questions below

Why is there a ₦100 application fee if the scholarship is free?

Our scholarship program is completely free. The nominal ₦100 one-time payment serves as a verification fee to ensure the integrity of our application process.

In recent years, we've seen a significant increase in automated bot registrations and spam applications. This small fee has helped us:

  • Reduce bot applications by approximately 85%
  • Ensure only serious, genuine candidates apply
  • Generate a unique, secure candidate ID for each applicant
  • Maintain a fair selection process for all

Unlike other platforms that charge thousands of Naira, our goal is accessibility while maintaining process integrity.

How long does the application process take?

The complete process from application to acceptance takes approximately 3 months. Here's a breakdown:

  • Week 1: Application submission and verification
  • Weeks 2-4: Initial screening and document review
  • Month 2: Detailed assessment and evaluation
  • Month 3: Final selection and notifications

We carefully review each application to ensure a fair selection process. This thorough approach helps us identify the most promising candidates.

How will I know if I've been selected?

Our selection team will contact successful applicants directly via the email address provided during registration. Here's our notification process:

  • Initial selection notifications are sent within 8 weeks of application submission
  • Formal acceptance letters follow within 2 weeks of initial notification
  • We also update your application status in your candidate portal
  • For urgent inquiries, you can contact our support team at selection@globalscholars.org

Important: Always check your spam/junk folder and ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

Is everyone eligible to apply?

Yes! Our scholarship is open to all Nigerian students who meet the academic requirements of our partner universities. We encourage applications from:

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students
  • All fields of study (STEM, Arts, Social Sciences, Business, etc.)
  • Students from all Nigerian states and regions
  • Both recent graduates and current students

The only requirements are Nigerian citizenship, academic excellence, and a demonstrated commitment to contributing to Nigeria's development upon completion of studies.

What are the academic requirements?

While specific requirements vary by program and university, here are our general academic standards:

  • Undergraduate: Minimum of 5 credits in WAEC/NECO including English and Mathematics
  • Postgraduate: Minimum Second Class Upper division or its equivalent
  • Strong personal statement demonstrating leadership potential
  • Evidence of extracurricular activities and community involvement

We take a holistic approach to evaluation, considering not just grades but also character, potential, and commitment to Nigeria's development.

How many applications do you receive and how many are selected?

Each year, we receive thousands of applications from talented Nigerian students across the country. Here are our recent statistics:

  • Annual Applications: Approximately 25,000
  • Scholarships Awarded: About 3,000 (12% acceptance rate)
  • Countries Available: 15+ destinations worldwide
  • Total Students Funded Since 2012: 5,200+

Our selective process ensures that only the most deserving candidates receive this life-changing opportunity. We carefully review each application to identify those with exceptional academic potential and leadership qualities.

What exactly is covered by the scholarship?

Our scholarship is truly fully-funded. If selected, we cover every essential cost:

  • Full Tuition: Complete program fees at prestigious universities
  • Accommodation: Comfortable housing near campus
  • Living Stipend: Monthly allowance for food and expenses
  • Travel: Round-trip flights to host country
  • Visa Fees: All visa application costs covered
  • Health Insurance: Comprehensive medical coverage
  • Books & Materials: Allowance for academic materials

Our goal is to ensure scholars can focus entirely on their studies without financial concerns.

What happens after I submit my application?

After submitting your application, here's what happens:

  1. Confirmation Email: You'll receive an acknowledgment with your unique candidate ID
  2. Screening: Our team reviews applications for eligibility and completeness
  3. Assessment: Detailed evaluation of academic records and personal statements
  4. Shortlisting: Top candidates are identified for further consideration
  5. Final Selection: Candidates are matched with universities and programs
  6. Notification: Successful applicants receive acceptance packages

You can check your application status anytime through your candidate portal using your unique ID.

Are there any other costs or hidden fees?

No. Our scholarship covers all essential costs as outlined. The only payment required is the one-time ₦100 verification fee during application.

Unlike other scholarship platforms, we don't charge:

  • Processing fees beyond the initial ₦100
  • Consultation or advisory fees
  • Documentation or certification fees
  • Any post-acceptance charges

We are transparent about all costs from the beginning. If selected, everything listed in our benefits section is covered at no additional cost to you.

Can I apply for multiple programs or countries?

Yes, you can indicate your preferences for up to 3 programs or countries in your application. However, we recommend:

  • Choosing programs that align with your academic background and career goals
  • Researching universities and countries that best fit your aspirations
  • Being open to alternatives as our matching process considers availability and fit

Our selection committee will match successful candidates with the most suitable opportunities based on their qualifications, preferences, and program availability.

Important Reminder

Our application process is designed to be fair and accessible. The ₦100 verification fee is our only charge and has significantly reduced fraudulent applications, ensuring genuine candidates receive proper attention.

Application Tip: Submit your application early, ensure all documents are clear and legible, and write a compelling personal statement that highlights your unique qualities and commitment to Nigeria's development.
