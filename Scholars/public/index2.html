About the Application Verification Fee

One-Time Application Verification

Applying for the Global Scholars Hub program is completely free.

The ₦100 payment is not for the scholarship. It is only used to verify that applications are submitted by real people, not automated bots or spam accounts.
In the past, thousands of fake applications made it difficult to review genuine students fairly. This small verification step helps protect serious applicants and keeps the process transparent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our scholarship program

Why is there a ₦100 payment if the scholarship is free?

The scholarship itself is completely free. The ₦100 payment is only for application verification. It helps us prevent fake or automated applications and ensures that every real applicant is reviewed fairly.

Who is eligible to apply?

This program is open to Nigerian students who meet basic academic and documentation requirements. Final placement depends on university criteria and available slots.

When will I know if I've been selected?

Selection notifications will be sent via email within 8 weeks of the application deadline. Please make sure your contact information is correct when you apply. We'll also post updates on our website.

What happens after I pay the ₦100 fee?

After payment confirmation, your application immediately enters our evaluation system. You'll receive a confirmation email with your application reference. The verification fee confirms application authenticity, ensuring fairness for all applicants.
