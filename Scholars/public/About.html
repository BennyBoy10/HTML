About Our Program

Since 2012, Global Scholars Hub has supported Nigerian students by providing them with fully funded international scholarship opportunities and guiding them through a clear, structured application process.

Our Mission & Journey

Our mission is to support Nigerian students with strong academic ability and clear potential by providing access to fully funded international education opportunities, regardless of financial background.

We focus on identifying committed students and guiding them through a transparent selection process, working with partner institutions that offer full financial support.

The program aims to equip scholars with global experience and practical skills that can contribute positively to their personal growth and Nigeria’s development.

What We Cover

The Global Scholars Hub program is designed to remove the major financial challenges associated with studying abroad by covering essential academic and living expenses required throughout the duration of the study program.

Full Tuition

Tuition fees for the entire academic program are covered and paid directly to the host institution. This ensures students can focus fully on their studies without concerns about school fees or unexpected academic charges.

Accommodation

Students are provided with safe and suitable accommodation located close to their campus. Housing arrangements are made to meet basic living standards and remain consistent throughout the study period.

Living Stipend

A monthly living allowance is provided to support basic daily expenses such as food, transportation, study materials, and personal needs, based on the cost of living in the host country.

Travel

The program covers international travel expenses, including a return flight between Nigeria and the host country at the beginning and end of the academic program.

Visa Fees

All standard student visa application costs required by the host country are covered, ensuring applicants are not financially restricted during the visa process.

Health Insurance

Comprehensive health insurance is provided for the duration of the program, allowing students to access medical care in line with the regulations of their host country.

Study Abroad Destinations

Scholars are placed in universities across different regions of the world based on program availability, academic background, and eligibility requirements set by partner institutions.

Verification Process

The Global Scholars Hub application is free. The ₦100 verification fee helps confirm real applicants and reduce duplicate or automated submissions.

₦100 Only

In recent years, a high number of automated and duplicate submissions made it difficult to properly review genuine applicants. The verification fee allows us to reduce these issues and focus attention on serious candidates who meet the program requirements.

Prevents Fake Applications
